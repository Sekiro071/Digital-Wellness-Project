Bitwarden

Generate, store, and use stronger unique passwords across devices instead of reusing one password.

Best for

Students juggling many accounts who want a repeatable password habit.

This is a fit guide, not a universal ranking.

Quick facts
  • Platforms: Web, Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS
  • Pricing: Free core; Premium features paid
  • Source checked: 17 Nov 2026
Privacy

Requires an account and a master password; review the current plan, encryption, recovery, and sync details.

Limitations

Free includes password storage, generator and sync. Integrated authenticator codes, file attachments and emergency access require Premium. Plan recovery for a forgotten master password.

Accessibility

Accessibility support has not been independently tested; check keyboard, screen reader and text scaling on your device.
