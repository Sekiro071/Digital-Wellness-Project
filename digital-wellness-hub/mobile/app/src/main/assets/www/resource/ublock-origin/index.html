uBlock Origin

Filter unwanted content and reduce exposure to advertising and tracking requests in supported browsers.

Best for

Students who want a capable browser-level privacy and content-filtering layer.

This is a fit guide, not a universal ranking.

Quick facts
  • Platforms: Firefox
  • Pricing: Free / open source
  • Source checked: 17 Nov 2026
Privacy

Open-source project; permissions and browser compatibility should be reviewed for the exact extension variant.

Limitations

Full uBlock Origin is a Firefox extension here. uBlock Origin Lite is a separate product for browsers including Chrome; neither filters other apps.

Accessibility

Accessibility support has not been independently tested; check keyboard, screen reader and text scaling on your device.

Your next step

Check the current official details.

Product features, permissions, pricing, and availability change. Verify the source before you install or enable anything.
