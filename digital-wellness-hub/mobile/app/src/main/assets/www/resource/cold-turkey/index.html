Cold Turkey Blocker

Block distracting websites and, with supported features, applications during scheduled work blocks.

Best for

Students who need firmer desktop boundaries than a browser-only extension provides.

This is a fit guide, not a universal ranking.

Quick facts
  • Platforms: Windows, macOS
  • Pricing: Free basic blocking; Pro is paid
  • Source checked: 17 Nov 2026
Privacy

Installation can require elevated permissions for blocking features; review the official requirements and policy.

Limitations

Free includes timed website blocks. Application blocking and scheduled blocks require paid Pro; Linux, ChromeOS, Android and iOS are unsupported.

Accessibility

Accessibility support has not been independently tested; check keyboard, screen reader and text scaling on your device.

Your next step

Check the current official details.

Product features, permissions, pricing, and availability change. Verify the source before you install or enable anything.
