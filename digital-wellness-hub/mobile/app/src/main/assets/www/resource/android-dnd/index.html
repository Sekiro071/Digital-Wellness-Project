Android Do Not Disturb

Silence interruptions, choose exceptions, and schedule calmer study or bedtime windows.

Best for

Android students who want to reduce notification noise without installing a new app.

This is a fit guide, not a universal ranking.

Quick facts
  • Platforms: Android
  • Pricing: Built-in
  • Source checked: 17 Nov 2026
Privacy

Built into Android. The exact settings and automation options vary by device and Android version.

Limitations

Some devices now surface this through Modes; emergency calls, alarms, and exceptions need careful review.

Accessibility

Accessibility support has not been independently tested; check keyboard, screen reader and text scaling on your device.
