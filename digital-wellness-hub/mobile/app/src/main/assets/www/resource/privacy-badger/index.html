Privacy Badger

Block known third-party trackers and optionally learn about additional trackers as you browse.

Best for

Students who want a low-maintenance tracker protection starting point.

This is a fit guide, not a universal ranking.

Quick facts
  • Platforms: Chrome, Firefox, Edge
  • Pricing: Free / open source
  • Source checked: 17 Nov 2026
Privacy

EFF says it does not share browsing data unless you choose to submit a broken-site report.

Limitations

Chrome on Android does not support extensions; a site may need a one-site exception if something breaks.

Accessibility

Accessibility support has not been independently tested; check keyboard, screen reader and text scaling on your device.
