Guide 03 / Quiet time

Choose what can interrupt you.

Set a short study window, check the people and apps you need, and know how to turn the mode off.

Choose your device

Keep the first setup simple.

Use the steps for your device. Exact labels and available options depend on its software version.

Android: Do Not Disturb

  1. Open Settings and find Modes or Do Not Disturb.Search Settings if the menu path shown on your phone is different.
  2. Review what can interrupt.Check allowed people, apps, alarms, and filtered notification display before turning it on.
  3. Choose a short duration or schedule.Android’s options vary by phone; choose a duration you can verify and change.
  4. Turn it on and check its status.Use Quick Settings or Settings to confirm the mode is active.
  5. Turn it off from Quick Settings.Return to the mode control and switch it off when you finish.

iPhone: Focus

  1. Open Settings → Focus.Choose Do Not Disturb, Work, Personal, or another existing Focus.
  2. Choose allowed people and apps.Review calls and notifications carefully so important contacts and alerts are not unintentionally silenced.
  3. Add a schedule if useful.Set a start and end time, or use Control Center to turn Focus on for a short test.
  4. Check the status, then switch it off.Open Control Center, tap Focus, and select the active mode to stop it. To prevent it turning on again, return to Settings → Focus → your mode and review or disable its schedule.
Expected result: Your chosen mode is active, the exceptions are understood, and you know where to turn it off.

The Apple reference shows an example interface. Verify the status and allowed interruptions on your own phone before relying on a scheduled Focus.

Before you start
