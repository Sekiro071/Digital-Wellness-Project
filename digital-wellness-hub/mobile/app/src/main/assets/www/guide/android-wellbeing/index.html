Guide 01 / Android

Set one realistic phone limit.

Check one usage pattern, then try an app timer or bedtime routine you can adjust.

Goal

Make one habit easier to notice.

Choose one app or evening routine. Treat the limit as an experiment, not a judgment.

Set an app timer

  1. Open Settings.Search for Digital Wellbeing if you do not see it in the list.
  2. Open Digital Wellbeing.Review the dashboard and choose one app you want to limit.
  3. Open App timers.Tap the timer control beside that app. Some work or school accounts may not support app timers.
  4. Choose a daily limit and confirm.Pick an amount that fits your day, then tap OK. App timers reset at midnight.
  5. Check the result.Return to Digital Wellbeing later to see whether the limit suits you. To undo it, return to App timers, select the app, and delete its timer. Your phone may label these controls differently.

Or set a bedtime routine

Open Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Bedtime mode → Bedtime routine. Choose a schedule or a charging window, then review the screen options before saving. The available choices differ by phone.

Expected result: You can find the timer or routine you set and know where to change it. Your menu may use different names.

If the setting is missing

  • Use the Settings search field for “Digital Wellbeing” or “App timers”.
  • Check whether a work or school profile restricts the feature.
  • Try a smaller change, such as one app or one short bedtime window.

The reference above is published by Google. The menu names on your own phone may differ.

Before you start

  • Android phone with Digital Wellbeing
  • Settings access
  • One app or sleep window to try
