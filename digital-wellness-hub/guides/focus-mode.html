Guide 03 / Quiet time

Create a quiet study mode.

Silence non-essential notifications for a study block or bedtime, while keeping the people and alarms you genuinely need.

Goal

Decide what is allowed to interrupt you.

Choose one platform for your final screenshots. These steps describe the shared decision flow; exact menus vary by device.

Steps

  1. Open Settings or quick settings.Find Focus, Do Not Disturb, Modes, or the equivalent control on your device.
  2. Choose the mode.Select an existing study, work, sleep, or Do Not Disturb mode, or create a custom one if your device supports it.
  3. Choose people who can interrupt.Allow essential contacts or calls according to your needs. Review repeated-call and emergency behavior.
  4. Choose apps that can interrupt.Allow only the apps that are genuinely needed during this mode.
  5. Add a schedule.Set a study time or bedtime routine if scheduling is available. Keep the first experiment short enough to test.
  6. Review visual and lock-screen options.Some devices can hide or delay silenced notifications, change a screen, or share a Focus status.
  7. Enable and verify.Turn the mode on and look for the status indicator. Send a test notification from a permitted and non-permitted app if practical.
  8. Learn how to turn it off.Return to quick settings or Settings so you know how to edit the schedule and exceptions later.
Expected result: You can activate the mode and understand which notifications are allowed. Exact steps differ between Android and Apple devices.

Troubleshooting

  • If you miss something important, adjust exceptions rather than abandoning the whole routine.
  • If a schedule does not trigger, confirm the device time zone, repeat pattern, and mode is enabled.
  • If you publish this guide, label the platform and OS version used for screenshots.

Before you start

  • Android or Apple device
  • One study or bedtime window
  • List of essential people or apps
