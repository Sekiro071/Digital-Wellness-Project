Guide 01 / Android

Set a healthier phone boundary.

Use Digital Wellbeing to understand one pattern, then set one realistic app timer or bedtime routine.

Goal

Make one phone habit visible and adjustable.

Start with curiosity rather than a punishment. Choose one app or one evening window you want to test.

Steps

  1. Open Settings.On your Android device, open the Settings app.
  2. Find Digital Wellbeing.Search Settings for “Digital Wellbeing” or open it from the settings list.
  3. Review the dashboard.Look at screen time, times opened, and notifications. Notice one pattern without judging it.
  4. Choose one app to limit.Open the app from the dashboard or find App timers, then select the app you want to experiment with.
  5. Set a realistic timer.Choose a limit you could actually keep. You can change or remove it later.
  6. Find Bedtime mode if you need it.Look for Bedtime mode or a related sleep routine. Device names and options vary.
  7. Choose the schedule and options.Set a start and end time, then review any screen or notification changes available on your device.
  8. Return and confirm.Check that the timer or routine is active. Test the dashboard again later to see what changed.
Expected result: You can find your usage information and have configured at least one limit or routine. A timer is a prompt, not a guarantee that an app will never be opened.

Troubleshooting

  • If the menu is named differently, use the Settings search field.
  • Some work or school accounts may restrict app timers.
  • If the timer does not fit, lower the scope: one app, one evening, one experiment.

Before you start

  • Supported Android phone
  • Settings access
  • One app or bedtime window in mind
