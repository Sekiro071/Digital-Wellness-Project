How to use the hub

A small decision, made clearer.

There’s no universal “best” tool. The right fit depends on the problem, the device in your hand, and the boundaries you can actually keep.

01 / NOTICE

Name the friction.

Start with the moment that keeps getting in the way: a buzzing phone, a tab spiral, or an account you keep reusing.

02 / COMPARE

Check the trade-offs.

Look at the platform, price, permissions, accessibility, and limitations before you install or enable anything.

03 / TRY

Make one change.

Use a guide, test the setting, and keep what helps. Tools support habits; they do not replace your judgment.
