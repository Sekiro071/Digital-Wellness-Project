The thinking behind the site

Clarity over hype.

Good Signal is a student-focused resource hub designed to help people choose digital wellness tools with more context and less marketing noise.

Audience

Built for a busy student day.

The core audience is college and university students, roughly 18–24, navigating study, social life, sleep, and many devices at once. The copy stays practical, non-judgmental, and clear about limits.

Method

Compare the same questions every time.

Each resource is described using a consistent frame: purpose, platform, pricing label, privacy requirements, accessibility, limitations, best-fit use case, and official source.

  • Use official product, help, pricing, and privacy pages first.
  • Separate confirmed details from the site's own assessment.
  • Show the date checked so the reader knows when to re-verify.
  • Prefer a built-in feature when it solves the problem simply.
Scope

Useful on purpose.

This hub combines a resource directory, transparent recommendations, setup guides, and a private focus timer. A small FastAPI server provides page navigation and accepts feedback. No account is needed.

Saved resources and focus history stay in your browser. Feedback answers are sent to the server and stored locally in its database. Do not include identifying details. The hub has no advertising or analytics; fonts may load from Google Fonts.

Project note

Keep the research alive.

Product names, prices, supported platforms, permissions, and privacy practices change. Re-check the official links on the date you publish or present the project.
